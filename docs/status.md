# TerminalGB — where it stands

**Field report · 20 August 2026.** A Game Boy that lives in your terminal: where it stands
against every public accuracy suite, what it can do, and what is coming next.

> **[GB Emulator Shootout](https://gbdev.io/GBEmulatorShootout/): 243 / 264 (92.0 %) — 5th of 19
> published emulators.** Measured through their manifest and their screenshot comparison, on
> `main`; the recipe is in [`docs/shootout.md`](shootout.md). It was 223 until `identical`
> became the default picture engine on 20 August 2026 — fifteen gains, zero losses.

**Two [picture modes](picture-modes.md), both gated in CI.** `identical` is the default since 2026-08-20 and runs the real hardware pixel pipeline dot by dot, scoring **75/75** on Mooneye `acceptance` and **29/79** on Mealybug; `standard` draws a whole scanline at a time for about an eighth of the PPU cost and is what small hardware and headless collectors should run. `same` above means the suite has one baseline because the mode does not reach what it measures (audio, DMA, mappers, cartridge clocks) — those three are gated in both modes against that one file. `identical` wins overwhelmingly and no longer loses `cgb-acid2`; four small Gambatte groups are still net-negative by eight rows between them, against 891 gains ([which rows, and how each moved](measured/scoreboard.md#the-identical-picture-mode-scores-differently--and-mostly-dominates)).

## The Mealybug number, in context

Three out of seventy-nine looks alarming until you see what everyone else scores. On the
twenty-four rows the Shootout publishes:

| Emulator | Rows passed | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| SameBoy | 15 | the reference implementation |
| **TerminalGB** | **3** | **pixel-exact, all three hardware sets** |
| bgb | 2 | long-standing, widely used |
| PyBoy | 0 | — |

It is not a suite a competent emulator sweeps. Passing a row needs a picture unit rebuilt to run
pixel by pixel rather than a scanline at a time — [that engine exists here and is **the default**
since 20 August 2026](picture-modes.md), scoring **<!--score:mealybug_accurate:pass-->29<!--/score-->/79**
against the cheap engine's <!--score:mealybug:pass-->3<!--/score-->. **45 of the 79 rows cannot be
won by picture work at all** — 27 need the boot ROM's `®` glyph, 8 compare our greyscale against a
colour reference and 7 are a ROM/reference mismatch in the suite — so the honest denominator is 34,
and [`docs/mealybug.md` §11](mealybug.md#11-the-denominator-45-of-the-failing-rows-cannot-be-won-by-ppu-work-15-aug-2026)
does the arithmetic.

## Abilities

### The machine

- DMG, Game Boy Color and [Super Game Boy](components/sgb.md) models
- Cartridge mappers 1, 2, 3 and 5, with a [real-time clock](measured/cartridges.md)
- Battery saves in the ecosystem's own format
- [Save states](savestates.md), versioned, portable to the browser
- Optional user-supplied [boot ROM](measured/boot.md) — *new*
- Two [picture modes](picture-modes.md): `standard`, or hardware-exact `identical` — *new*

### In your terminal

- Kitty, Sixel, iTerm2 and half-block [rendering](rendering.md)
- Shared-memory frame transport — 87 bytes a frame
- Four [upscalers](upscaling.md), palettes, HiDPI correction
- Runs in any terminal; degrades, never refuses

### Sound

- Full [audio hardware emulation](audio.md), 36 ms latency
- Volume control that never touches emulation
- [Chiptune renderer](chiptune.md) — instructions in, real audio out — *new*
- [Music mode](music-mode.md): ROMs as a shelf, with export — *new*

### Linking

- [Two consoles on one cable](link-cable.md), trade proven — *new*
- [BGB network protocol over TCP](bgb-link.md) — *new*
- [Virtual trade partner](plugins.md) — no second cartridge needed
- [Cable Club battles](link-battle.md) against the partner

### Plugins

- [Live trainer view](trainer.md): party, stats, sprites, catch rate
- [Shiny hunting](shiny-hunt.md) from Gen 1 hidden values — *new*
- [Battle control](gen1/battle-control.md) — start any battle from outside
- [AI agent substrate](claude-plays.md) for autonomous play

### Beyond the terminal

- [Virtual camera](video-out.md) — appears as a webcam
- Screenshots, recording, [telemetry stream](telemetry.md)
- [MCP server](mcp.md): any Claude client can drive it
- Embeddable core — one third-party dependency
- Browser build, and a [conformance action](conformance-action.md) others can use

## Known gaps, stated plainly

> [!WARNING]
> **Mid-scanline picture timing** is still the real ceiling, even now that the pixel-by-pixel
> engine is [the default](picture-modes.md). Most of Mealybug is red in that mode too — though
> **45 of the 79 rows cannot be won by PPU work at all**; see
> [`docs/mealybug.md` §11](mealybug.md#11-the-denominator-45-of-the-failing-rows-cannot-be-won-by-ppu-work-15-aug-2026)
> for the denominator and what each remaining row would take. The cheap whole-scanline engine is
> still one keypress away for small hardware and for headless throughput.

> [!NOTE]
> **Nine Mooneye rows are other consoles** — machine variants we deliberately do not emulate.
> Pinned as failures on purpose rather than quietly dropped from the denominator.

> [!NOTE]
> **Four SameSuite rows** — two target older Color revisions and a Game Boy Advance. One is
> unsolved by the suite author's own emulator, whose source says the logic behind it is still
> unclear. [Each red row's evidence](measured/scoreboard.md).

> [!NOTE]
> **One test must stay red.** The Color memory-corruption defect does not exist in Color
> silicon, so real hardware fails that ROM too. Passing it would be the bug.
