## Why it moved

This page was always "the map of the subject" — a table pointing at where every battle-related
fact lived, plus the one thing that had no home of its own: what a battle screen shows. That
last part is a fact about Pokémon Red and Blue, not about this emulator, so it belongs where
AtlasGB already keeps the rest of the battle chapter.
